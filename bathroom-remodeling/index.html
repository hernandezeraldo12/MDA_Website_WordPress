PROCESS

HERE IS HOW THE PROCESS WORKS

A clear, step-by-step process designed to make your bathroom remodeling experience smooth, organized, and stress-free.

01

INITIAL CONSULTATION

We start by learning about your bathroom, your goals, your needs, and the changes you'd like to make. We discuss your ideas, priorities, and the overall scope of your remodeling project.

02

DESIGN & PLANNING

Our team develops a bathroom design around your space and lifestyle. We work through the layout, functionality, fixtures, tile patterns, materials, and other details before construction begins.

03

SELECTIONS & PREPARATION

Once the design takes shape, we finalize key materials, tiles, vanities, and project details. We also prepare necessary plans, coordinate required permits, and get the space ready for work.

04

DEMOLITION & CONSTRUCTION

With the project prepared, construction begins. We carefully remove existing elements as needed and move into remodeling, including structural framing, plumbing, and electrical upgrades.

05

INSTALLATION & FINISHING

Your new bathroom comes together with tile installation, shower and tub fitting, vanity setups, lighting, mirrors, and custom finishing details according to the approved design plan.

06

FINAL WALKTHROUGH

Before completing the project, we review the finished bathroom with you, verifying every single detail to ensure everything is spotless, fully functional, and ready for daily use.
