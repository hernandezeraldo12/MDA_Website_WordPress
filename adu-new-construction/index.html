PROCESS

HERE IS HOW THE PROCESS WORKS

A clear, step-by-step process designed to make your ADU journey smooth, transparent, and stress-free.

01

INITIAL CONSULTATION

We begin by learning how you want to use your ADU, what you hope to accomplish, and what you have in mind for the space. We'll discuss your goals, budget, and property so we can determine the right direction for your project.

02

DESIGN & PLANNING

Our team works with you to develop a functional ADU layout that fits your property and your needs. We consider the available space, desired features, functionality, and overall design to create a plan that feels like a natural part of your property.

03

PERMITS & APPROVALS

Before construction begins, We help coordinate the required plans, permits, and approvals for your ADU project. Our team works through the necessary local requirements so the project is properly prepared before construction begins.

04

SITE PREPARATION & CONSTRUCTION

Once the necessary approvals are in place, construction begins. Our team prepares the site and coordinates the major stages of the build, from the structural work and framing to electrical, plumbing, insulation, flooring, and other required installations.

05

FINISHING & INSPECTIONS

As construction nears completion, we focus on the finishing details and required inspections. From fixtures and finishes to the final details, we make sure the completed space is ready for the final walkthrough.

06

FINAL WALKTHROUGH & COMPLETION

We walk through the completed ADU with you to review the finished space and address any final details. Once everything is complete, we'll provide the necessary documentation and help you move forward with your new space.
